# 0.6.2 controls, scrolling and iOS video layering — 2026-11-15

## Changes

- Samsung + Windows + Android TV: poster activation transfers focus to the primary play/resume action. Asynchronous series loading preserves a single pending focus transfer. Selection itself does not start playback. Native touch pages retain their own full-page flow.
- Samsung and native player keys: file/episode Left/Right seek exactly ten seconds until Up/Down enters menu navigation. Auto-hide resets navigation mode. Volume remains on +/- and controls; live channel keys remain separate. Loading AVPlay queues seek deltas and initializes duration/time before applying them.
- Samsung: compositor/browser smooth scrolling with coalesced destinations and reduced-motion support; virtual lists render per row, not pixel. Channel focus uses logical indices and bounded overscan, preserving fast repeated key presses. Native focus scrolling keeps egui animation and only scrolls as far as needed to expose the focused item.
- iOS: explicit native video/root Metal z-order and sibling-order invariant, reapplied around VLC asynchronous view creation/layout. The video drawable does not intercept touch. This addresses the reported video-over-overlay regression; no iPhone is attached for direct visual confirmation.

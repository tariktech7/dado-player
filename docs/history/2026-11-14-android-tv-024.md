The published 0.2.3 TV mode was tested at the Android TV reference canvas, 960 × 540 dp. It exposed wrapped category headings, a clipped Settings entry and oversized combined columns. The corrected TV mode uses a narrower navigation rail, smaller row spacing, density-independent type and bounded column widths. Current EPG has space below the preview; redundant TV playback buttons were replaced by a remote hint and a favorite icon. TV branding now uses the shared D7 asset.

Testing Back at the task root then reopening exposed an ANR: finishing GameActivity attempted to recreate winit's process-wide event loop. Root Back now backgrounds the task. Three leave/reopen cycles retained PID 5895 and remained interactive; Settings was then reached with six Down presses and OK.

40 Rust tests and root Clippy passed, including TV geometry with all four columns and the Settings label inside safe bounds at 960 × 540 and 1280 × 720. Native APK built, v2/v3 project signature and alignment verified, update installed successfully.

Real app captures were made at 1920 × 1080 / 320 dpi and 3840 × 2160 / 640 dpi. Both represent a logical 960 × 540 canvas. Directional navigation, first OK for preview, preview OK for player, Back and Settings were exercised. These were an Android x86_64 emulator running the arm64 APK with TV mode forced on, not real Android-TV hardware. Its phone system bars may appear in captures.
